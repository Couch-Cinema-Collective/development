# Couch Cinema Collective

**A film society with an awards night — not a fantasy league with a standings table.**

## Overview

Couch Cinema Collective turns watching movies with friends into a season with real structure. Members join a small guild (capped at 50 people) that runs on a season clock: nominate films, watch them together, vote, and close it out with a full awards ceremony. Recognition isn't a made-up point score — it's a fact about the season: the awards *your* nominations actually won.

The tone is intentionally closer to a serious film club than a game — institutional, black/white/red, PBS-style — with just enough mechanics to make nominating feel like something you're playing, not just a poll.

## How a Season Works

1. **Nominate** — Every member gets 5 points to spend across films: all-in on one pick, spread thin across five, or anything in between. Points from the whole guild stack on shared titles, and the highest totals make the slate. Who-nominated-what stays hidden until lock, so it's real conviction, not groupthink.
2. **Watch** — The locked slate (ties expand it — nobody's pick dies on a technicality) gets watched together on the guild's own timeline, with progress tracking and member reviews along the way.
3. **Vote** — Once you've finished the slate, you fill out a ballot — one pick per award category. (Commissioners can require honest self-attestation, verified watch history, or leave it open.)
4. **Ceremony** — An Oscar-night reveal, category by category. Every member who nominated the winning film gets full credit for that award — no splitting points, no penalty for betting on a popular pick. Sweeps are allowed; if the guild loves one film, it can win everything.

## How to Get Involved

- **Join a guild** — Get an invite link from a commissioner (the member running that season), or start your own and invite up to 49 others.
- **Make your picks** — Search and nominate straight from a live film catalog: posters, cast, runtime, and where to stream each title.
- **Stay in the loop on Discord** — Draft openings, lock warnings, slate reveals, voting windows, and winners all post to your guild's server automatically.
- **Explore the film wiki** — No membership required. Browse genre and movement write-ups with canonical examples — the open front door to the Collective.
